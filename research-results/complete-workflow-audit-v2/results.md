# Complete workflow audit v2 — result

**Decision:** `RESEARCH_WORKFLOW_FINISHED_ARTIFACT_NOT_SUPPORTED_FILAMENT`

The complete-workflow comparison does not support making the research runtime the default path for ordinary filament work.

## Finished-artifact outcome

The simplified workflow completed **12/12** verified posts. It used **16 exploratory candidate renders total** across the 12 briefs (mean `1.33`): nine briefs stopped at candidate 1, F08/F10 at candidate 2, and F05 at candidate 3.

The research arm spent the frozen **20 generated attempts per brief** (`10 native + 10 spectral`; 240 attempts total), then used the supported target-blind three-item max-dispersion delivery surface.

- 9/12 delivered shortlists contained a brief-adherent candidate selected for deployment.
- F02, F06 and F07 had no brief-adherent delivered candidate.
- After the blinded shortlist choices were frozen and unblinded, **all 9 selected candidates were spectral**.
- Both allowed compression attempts for all 9 selected candidates passed exact runtime and <=280 raw/X-weighted length.
- Neither compression attempt preserved enough of the selected expanded spectral identity to satisfy Grade 0; therefore none could satisfy Grade 1.
- Research-arm verified deployment completion: **0/12**.

There is consequently no valid final-artifact A/B package: the research arm has no Grade-1 deployment artifact to pair with the 12 simple-arm finals. The preregistered advancement gate therefore fails before final human pairwise review.

## What failed

Tweet length was **not** the binding failure. Every attempted compact R post fit and executed.

The recurring loss was representational: the selected expanded candidates used a multi-mode K=2 Hamiltonian spectral material field. A one-mode compact reduction fit easily but collapsed the selected material into a generic smooth spine. The second and final allowed attempt retained two Hamiltonian spectral displacement modes and all previously named traveling-phase/harmonic relationships, but the observable selected material still did not survive.

This is the strongest supported diagnosis from the audit:

```text
good expanded candidate
→ selected successfully
→ exact compact code fits
→ selected material identity is lost
```

That is `COMPRESSION_REPRESENTATION`, not a character-count problem and not evidence for a better search policy.

## Bottleneck audit

`COMPRESSION_REPRESENTATION`:

`NO_SINGLE_BOTTLENECK`:

- F02, F06, F07

For the latter three, the delivered shortlist had no brief-adherent candidate. The authoritative R artifact preserved the shortlist and sealed mapping but **did not preserve the complete valid archive for later artistic inspection**. Regenerating the consumed `768xxx` population would violate custody, so generation versus delivery cannot be separated retrospectively. The primary finished-artifact conclusion remains valid; only this fine-grained diagnosis is limited.

## Efficiency observation

The simple workflow reached 12 deployable results with 16 exploratory renders. The research arm used 240 generated attempts before delivery plus 18 deployment-compression attempts and still produced no deployable final artifact under the frozen preservation contract.

This does not mean the research search is artistically useless. The fact that all nine selected expanded candidates were spectral is evidence that the supported spectral material operator can expose visually useful expanded forms. It means the present end-to-end system cannot **carry those gains through the compact deployment boundary**.

## Roadmap consequence

The active research surface moves to **representation and compression**: find reusable compact encodings that preserve the recurring material/cause lost here. Do not respond by adding more search machinery or another automatic judge.

The recurrent/Sotaku-inspired learned transition remains parked. This audit did not show that useful representations stall because existing transitions are the limiting factor; the strongest supported bottleneck is downstream at compact representation.

The research runtime remains optional for ordinary filament work. The simplified workflow remains the production baseline.
