# Compact material grammar portfolio v1 — result

Decision: `COMPACT_MATERIAL_GRAMMAR_PORTFOLIO_ARTISTIC_NOT_SUPPORTED`.

The experiment tested whether deployment-native compact material becomes useful when offered as optional search material instead of being forced onto every incumbent.

## Frozen comparison

Across 12 fresh `771xxx` seeds:

- `NATIVE12`: 12 compact native filament posts;
- `MIXED12`: the exact first six native posts plus 2 CHIRP, 2 EPICYCLE, and 2 COUNTER posts;
- both arms used the same target-blind max-dispersion three-item delivery rule;
- all 216 unique generated candidates were complete executable <=280-character posts before delivery;
- the full generated archive was preserved before review.

All hard preconditions passed.

## Blinded artistic result

Human judgments were committed before the sealed archive/key was opened.

- reviewable: 12/12
- decisive: 12/12
- MIXED12 wins: 4
- NATIVE12 wins: 8
- MIXED12 decisive win rate: 33.33%
- one-sided exact binomial p: 0.927001953125

The preregistered advancement gate fails strongly.

## Post-hoc diagnostic

Because the full archive was preserved, delivery exposure can be checked without regenerating the consumed population.

- 10/12 mixed max-dispersion shortlists contained at least one material candidate;
- only 2/12 mixed shortlists were all-native;
- the 36 delivered mixed slots contained 18 native, 8 EPICYCLE, 5 CHIRP, and 5 COUNTER candidates;
- all four MIXED12 wins contained material in the delivered shortlist.

Therefore the negative portfolio result is not explained by the delivery reducer simply hiding the new material grammar.

These are portfolio-level labels. They do **not** support post-hoc mechanism ranking or parameter tuning.

## Consequence

Close the exact CHIRP/EPICYCLE/COUNTER grammar and 6-native/6-material allocation. Do not tune it against consumed `771xxx` ratings.

Together with `compact-symplectic-material-v1` and `phase-native-material-v1`, this closes the immediate line of shallow low-order compact enrichment. The next representation study should seek substantially more expressive structure per character rather than another additive compact modifier.

A promising materially different axis is **algorithmic depth**: a compact stateful recurrence along the open filament whose repeated nonlinear update creates structure from a small law. This remains a mathematical representation hypothesis, not a learned recurrent search operator.
