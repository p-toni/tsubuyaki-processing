# v0.4.1 Mitigation Validation

This note records the experiment that motivated and validated the v0.4.1 measurement changes.

## Test organism

A cold-ish v0.4 run used a compound manta/moth-like organism with:

- a central root mass;
- bilateral wing family;
- three trailing filaments;
- a central negative-space cavity;
- named controls: `wingSpan`, `tailLength`, `foldFrequency`, `bodyWidth`.

The readable form was structurally stronger than the exact tweet-ready phenotype, making it useful for testing both semantic-control measurement and morphology survival during golf.

## Finding 1 — static masks falsely penalized moving geometry

With baseline-only region support, correct geometry controls appeared to have very high spill because their new pixel positions fell outside the baseline mask.

Observed v0.4 static-mask results were approximately:

| control | declared scope | static-mask spill |
|---|---|---:|
| `wingSpan` | `region:wings` | 51.3% |
| `tailLength` | `region:tail` | 41.8% |
| `foldFrequency` | `surface:wings` | 47.1% |
| `bodyWidth` | `subtree:root` | 49.7% |

Using the union of baseline + variant support produced:

| control | dual-state spill | thresholded changed pixels inside allowed support |
|---|---:|---:|
| `wingSpan` | **8.1%** | 91.0% |
| `tailLength` | **3.0%** | 95.5% |
| `foldFrequency` | **7.0%** | 92.1% |
| `bodyWidth` | **12.1%** | 89.5% |

The earlier multi-frame experiment also found these corrected spill ranges remained stable:

- `wingSpan`: ~7.6–8.1%
- `tailLength`: ~2.6–3.3%
- `foldFrequency`: ~6.4–7.1%
- `bodyWidth`: ~12.1–12.2%

These are empirical values for this organism, **not universal thresholds**.

## Adversarial discrimination check

To verify the union was not merely making every control look local, an unrelated brightness/geometry change was deliberately added outside the wing support while evaluating `wingSpan`.

Result:

- normal dual-state `wingSpan` spill: **8.1%**
- deliberately smeared `wingSpan` spill: **56.2%**
- allowed changed-pixel share dropped from ~91% to ~48%

This is the behavior the semantic-scope metric is intended to provide: tolerate legitimate motion of the declared anatomy while still detecting unrelated influence.

## Finding 2 — global visual parity hid morphology loss during golf

The readable and exact tweet-ready forms both had healthy global visual metrics, yet visual inspection showed that the golfed phenotype simplified the body plan and weakened the central cavity/region differentiation.

Feature-wise probing over readable-region masks found approximately:

| feature | kind | golfed contrast-energy retention | visible-fraction change |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| root mass | presence | 80% | -50 pp |
| wing family | presence | 78% | -57 pp |
| tail family | presence | 98% | -55 pp |

The tail retained nearly all contrast energy but occupied it differently, illustrating why one scalar retention value is insufficient.

A manually declared central-cavity probe showed the golfed output increased contrast inside the intended void:

- visible fraction in cavity mask: **+6.2 percentage points**
- mean contrast in cavity mask: **+12.5** luminance units

That aligns with the visual observation that the cavity was being filled/erased during compression.

Again, these values are evidence for the feature-wise diagnostic design, not calibrated pass/fail limits.

## Resulting v0.4.1 rules

1. Geometry-control locality should use **baseline ∪ variant** region support.
2. `subtree` uses that dual-state union for the parent and every descendant.
3. Small mask dilation is only a raster tolerance and must remain visible in the report.
4. Compound expanded→golfed validation should declare **defining features**, not rely on occupancy/bbox parity.
5. Positive anatomy and intentional negative space need different measurements.
6. A release benchmark should include an intentionally smeared control to verify the metric still discriminates.

## Continuous optimization

`pi-autoresearch` was inspected as a possible long-running edit → benchmark → keep/revert loop. Its infrastructure is a strong future fit, but it is intentionally not wired into v0.4.1 because the current metrics are still being calibrated.

Before autonomous optimization, the objective should be robust against obvious proxy gaming such as:

- broadening region masks;
- weakening a control until spill approaches zero;
- preserving feature pixels while degrading overall artistic quality;
- trading away topology/authenticity to improve one numerical metric.

The repository should first accumulate broader benchmark evidence across compound, 1D filament, iterative-attractor and abstract-sheet routes.
