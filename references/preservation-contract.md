# Discovery and preservation contract

The system performs two different jobs and must not collapse them into one notion of search.

```text
discovery
= plural: several mutually different mathematical answers may all be valuable

preservation
= univocal relative to one selected elite: the deployed compact form must preserve the relationships for which that elite was selected
```

This is an operational boundary, not a new fitness function and not a formal model-theoretic axiomatization of sketches.

## 1. Discovery remains plural

During creative discovery:

- expose multiple representations and niches;
- permit several non-equivalent candidates to remain live;
- use objective structural targets only as experimental instruments for search mechanics;
- use human / independent evidence for artistic promotion authority;
- do not require candidates to agree with one another merely because they satisfy the same brief.

A brief can have many good mathematical realizations.

## 2. Preservation begins only after an elite is selected

Once a candidate is selected for deployment, freeze an **observation contract** before compression:

- meaningful behavioral horizon `H`;
- sampled verification frames inside `H`;
- named defining mathematical relationships;
- brief invariants that the selected phenotype must continue to satisfy.

The compact artifact is judged relative to this frozen contract. The contract may be strengthened after a demonstrated false pass; it may not be weakened post hoc to rescue a compact artifact.

## 3. Two preservation grades

### Grade 0 — observation-preserving

The compact artifact matches the frozen observable checks over `H`: valid rendering, sampled-frame behavior, support/occupancy or other qualified phenotype measurements.

Grade 0 is necessary but weak. A different mechanism can reproduce the same finite observations.

### Grade 1 — cause-preserving

Grade 0 passes **and** every named defining relationship remains explicitly present or recoverably defined in the compact construction.

Examples:

- recurrence/residue relation is still the source of the repeated structure;
- a shared family law still generates the siblings;
- a true 2D sampling/fold relationship is not replaced by an unrelated silhouette hack;
- a traveling axial phase relationship still generates the motion;
- a required morphology parent/attachment relationship remains causal rather than merely visually imitated.

Only Grade 1 is eligible for deployment promotion.

```text
Grade 0 != promotion
Grade 1 = minimum deployment preservation contract
```

Do not interpret Grade 1 as a claim of formal definitional equivalence between first-order theories. It is the project's concrete cause-preservation requirement.

## 4. Mechanical verification is a veto, not an intention certificate

Exact verification may establish that the compact artifact satisfies the frozen observation and cause-preservation contract. It cannot establish that the contract captured the intended artwork or that the selected elite was artistically correct.

Therefore:

```text
mechanical failure -> veto deployment
mechanical pass -> eligible to preserve the prior artistic decision
```

It does not replace human / independent artistic authority.

## 5. Observation metrics cannot substitute for cause preservation

Occupancy, pixel/shape distance, prototype F1, learned visual descriptors and finite sampled frames can be legitimate diagnostics or search-research instruments. None alone proves identity preservation.

Do not promote a compact artifact because it merely reconstructs those observations while dropping a named defining relationship.

## 6. Future research: mutation-signature saturation

A later experiment may test whether stage unlocks can be triggered by candidates becoming review-indistinguishable under the **currently available mutation classes**, rather than by a fixed candidate count.

Adding a mutation class changes the available intervention vocabulary and may split candidates that were previously indistinguishable. Until that mechanism is experimentally validated, current review-bound unlock evidence remains authoritative; do not introduce an automatic indiscernibility score into production search.
