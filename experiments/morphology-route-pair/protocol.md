# Morphology route activation pair — internal cold pilot

Frozen before candidate generation.

## Purpose

Test both sides of the v0.14.1 routing boundary:

1. morphology-first should activate when the brief requires explicit differentiated anatomy, attachment hierarchy and local semantic controls;
2. morphology-first should **not** activate merely because a compact mathematical form looks animal-like.

This is an internal pilot, not an independent external replication.

## Brief A — positive morphology control

> Create an original translucent compound creature with a differentiated root mass, a visible central split/cavity, and an attached repeated organ family. Expose three named controls: one local to the root mass, one affecting the repeated organ family coherently, and one global motion control. The hierarchy and local editing behavior are part of the requested phenotype.

Expected route:

```text
morphology-first explicit anatomy
+ controllability
```

Required representation properties:

- morphology contract before search;
- root region / cavity / repeated attached family are semantically distinct;
- repeated organs come from one reusable generator;
- attachment is parent-relative;
- local root control does not materially reshape the repeated family;
- family control acts coherently across siblings;
- global motion control may affect the whole system.

A single undifferentiated harmonic blob that merely resembles a creature is a route failure for this brief.

## Brief B — negative morphology control

> Create an original translucent drifting form from one continuous compact mathematical generator. It may read as ray-like, jellyfish-like or otherwise animal-like, with bilateral spread and a trailing impression, but no named body regions or local anatomical editing are requested. The apparent organism should emerge from the mathematics rather than from explicit parts.

Expected route:

```text
math-first emergent form
```

Required representation properties:

- one compact kernel / latent field / family or projection law;
- no morphology graph;
- no separately authored wing/tail/head/body generators;
- apparent bilateral and trailing structure may emerge from phase, residue, deformation or projection;
- scene-graph machinery is a failure unless later evidence shows the compact generator cannot satisfy the brief.

## Matched evaluation

For both briefs inspect a meaningful temporal horizon and record:

- chosen route;
- representation plan;
- whether the route matches the brief semantics;
- iterations to viable form;
- visual/temporal coherence;
- whether complexity comes from shared causes rather than decorative independent effects;
- any route repair before search.

For Brief A additionally record:

- local root-control spill into the repeated family;
- repeated-family directional coherence;
- hierarchy/attachment survival under motion.

For Brief B additionally record:

- number of independently authored anatomical parts: target `0`;
- whether animal-like identity survives without a scene graph;
- whether morphology machinery was unnecessarily activated.

## Deployment boundary

Character count and tweet viability are excluded from route choice and artistic ranking.

If a candidate later reaches deployment, v0.14.1 compression-survival rules apply normally. This pilot's primary question is representation routing, not golf success.

## Experimental hygiene

- tag both briefs `novel` relative to this release test;
- evaluator: current ChatGPT model instance, not independent;
- do not use this pilot as proof of cross-evaluator generalization;
- preserve candidate parameters/seeds for reproduction;
- do not modify production policy from a single paired run.
