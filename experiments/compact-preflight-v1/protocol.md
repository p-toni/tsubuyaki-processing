# Compact preflight timing v1 — prospective protocol

## Decision

Does checking an already worthwhile sketch's compact feasibility while readable
exploration budget remains produce more worthwhile, verified deliveries than
checking only after readable exploration closes?

This is a four-brief engineering comparison with one author/reviewer in shared
context. The same agent writes the briefs and both arms and may remember prior
solutions. Commitments prevent retrospective edits, not that contamination.
Results are provisional model judgments, not independent validation, a clean
causal estimate, or a general success rate. This experiment does not change the
production skill's visual-ranking policy.

## Fresh outcome briefs

- **P01 Aperture:** An opaque folded annular membrane opens and closes an
  irregular central aperture, revealing a moving layer behind it. The foreground
  must hide the rear layer where they overlap. Local folds must deform; a rigid
  spinning ring, transparent overlay, or hole that never changes fails.
- **P02 Thread:** A broad undulating ribbon passes through a closed solid loop.
  The near part of the loop occludes the ribbon and the ribbon occludes the far
  part at their projected crossings. Motion must travel along the ribbon, with
  readable material width. Transparent crossings or two unrelated wires fail.
- **P03 Relay:** At least five rooted soft units receive a localized bending
  event in sequence across the group. Bases stay fixed, each unit deforms, and
  neighboring response peaks occur at visibly different times. Uniform global
  swaying, rigid translation, or unrelated flicker fails.
- **P04 Wake:** A visible moving exciter crosses a finite membrane. A localized
  deformation follows its recent passage with a visible lag, then the affected
  material relaxes. The exciter and wake must share their cause. A decorative
  trail, uniform sheet oscillation, or a ridge that simply moves in lockstep
  with the exciter fails.

These briefs were designed for this run, not independently held out. They do not
reuse the completed comparison's candidates. Formula choice remains free.

## Arms, order, and stopping

Each arm has at most **four readable candidates and two compact attempts per
brief**. A behavioral change is a readable candidate; a complete compact program
written for testing is a compact attempt, even if it fails syntax or length.
Preflights count, including unsuccessful ones. Preserve every attempted program.
No hidden sweeps, uncharged golf drafts, or post-selection behavior changes.

Commit this protocol before candidate construction. Commit four shared initial
sources before any rendering. First complete the late arm's readable trajectories
for all four briefs, using rendered observations as usual but no compact code or
character counts. Freeze its selections, artistic grades, brief requirements,
defining relationships, and ranked eligible fallbacks before any compact attempt
in either arm. A late run may stop after its first worthwhile candidate when no
specific useful improvement is identified; four candidates are a cap, not a quota.

Then run the early arm from the same initial sources and images. It may reuse a
late candidate, charged against its own allowance. Review and freeze each
candidate's artistic judgment before compacting it. Once the first candidate
meets the brief and earns A/B, make an exact compact attempt **before** any further
readable refinement. A failure may inform a readable representation change while
budget remains, or a second syntax-only compact attempt. A new representation
must retain the brief requirements and earn A/B on its own before its attempt.

A passing early compact candidate can be kept as incumbent. Stop when no concrete
worthwhile improvement remains; do not spend the full allowance automatically.
If a second attempt is used on a challenger, its failure cannot erase the already
verified incumbent. If no A/B candidate is found, stop without compression.

The late arm now makes up to two exact compact attempts on its frozen selections
and eligible fallbacks. It cannot create a new readable candidate after seeing
compression results. Identical source/post checks can be reused across arms but
are charged to each arm's budget. Record physical and logical effort separately.

This order protects the late arm from observed compact feedback, but gives the
early arm knowledge of the completed late exploration. Report that limitation.
Do not present the arms as blinded or independent. A timing policy changes which
decisions receive feasibility feedback; it does not receive extra attempts.

## Observation and technical contract

Use full p5.js 1.11.3 at 400×400 and frames 1–720, 60 fps (12 seconds). Each brief
must show its stated event over this frozen interval: opening/closing, traveling
ribbon motion, sequential response, or passage/relaxation. Do not rescue a failure
by changing the horizon. Review sixteen equally spaced stills, plus full-size
frames when a required relationship is unclear. Record visual review separately
from full numerical execution and playback checks.

Require finite coordinates/styles, a stable point count with fixed indexed
identity, at least 95% of point centers in frame, and no indexed point moving
more than 10 pixels per frame. There is no recycling exception in this study.
These checks constrain technical validity, not artistic ranking.

Every successful complete post, including its hashtag, must fit both 280 raw
Unicode code points and 280 X-weighted characters and execute without browser
errors. Preserve its selected readable coordinates and drawing styles on every
frame to tolerance 1e-7, and require byte-identical sampled readable/compact PNGs
in the same raster runtime. Explicitly check that the named defining relationships
remain in the compact construction; numerical agreement alone is insufficient.
If the exact preflight covers all checks, reuse it for delivery without another
attempt. Infrastructure fixes are logged and do not consume creative allowance.

## Review and advancement

A = distinctive finished work; B = coherent interesting study worth keeping;
C = functioning demonstration; D = broken/incoherent. For every inspected
candidate record brief requirements as met, unmet, or uncertain. A run succeeds
only with all met, A/B quality, and verified compact delivery. Uncertainty does
not count as adherence. Aesthetic grades remain model-only and cannot be revised
upward after a length pass.

An arm wins a pair when it alone succeeds. If both succeed, require a clear named
artistic advantage to prefer one; otherwise record equivalent. If neither
succeeds, record both-fail. Also compare readable outcomes and record any clear
artistic regression of an early delivery relative to the late A/B readable
selection, including when that late selection cannot be compacted.

The practical advancement gate requires **at least two early pair wins, zero late
wins, no fewer early deliveries, and no clear artistic regressions**. This is not
statistical significance. Passing supports a fresh replication before changing
the production default. Failing leaves the current process in place and calls
for diagnosing the observed failure rather than expanding machinery.

Report all four briefs, candidate and compact-attempt counts, logical versus
physical renders, known execution times, unmeasured active effort, and any human
creative intervention. Do not infer equal total cost from equal caps. Freeze the
results before changing documentation. Close this population after the result;
do not improve its recorded score with later repairs.
