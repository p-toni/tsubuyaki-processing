# Thirst — visual selection

I chose a feather and two equal drops of ink. A dry pair of barbs draws ink out of a drop. A wet pair lets it pass. The first drop can change how much of the second reaches the tip.

**V1, provisional C.** The first drop dwindles into the spine. The next keeps its size over the blue section and eventually falls clear. Each pair of barbs changes colour along its entire length in one frame. The straight-moving boundary makes the feather look like a progress indicator. [First sketch](v1.js)

**V2, provisional B.** Keep the revision. Indigo spreads out from the spine along the curved barbs. The last few tips keep darkening after the first drop has disappeared. On its return, the bead moves quickly over the blue section, slows when it reaches dry fibers, and falls from the end. At matched frame 268 of each encounter the first drop has gone and the second hangs in open space. The bead holds a perfectly circular outline even as it falls, which gives it some of the rigidity of a marble. [Selected sketch](v2.js)

The revision stores the time each pair took up ink. Those entries determine the wet section, how far the dye has traveled along each barb, and whether the bead loses ink and speed. Preserve those relationships, the identical incoming drops, and the falling remainder through both encounters. Clear the stored entries immediately before the second dose for the reset test.
