# Second encounter

**Make the second encounter visibly different because of what the first left behind.** The agent chooses the forms, material, and cause. The two encounters receive the same external input. Look for a change in what happens during the second encounter as well as a visible trace of the first. [Roadmap at the start of the study](https://github.com/p-toni/tsubuyaki-processing/blob/934637435cded1c5324d926f3ba5e496f6f9b791/references/research-roadmap.md)

Allow up to two readable sketches and two compact attempts. Review rendered motion and record the selection before writing or counting compact code. Give a provisional B to a piece worth keeping and an A to distinctive finished work. A mechanism that works but has little visual interest earns at most C.

Review 720 consecutive frames at 400 × 400 and an encoded 60 fps in p5.js 1.11.3. After visual selection, clear only the memory of the first encounter before replaying the second. Compare matched phases of the first, second, and reset encounters. Verify the exact compact sketch against every readable frame and in ordinary p5 playback. Aim for 280 characters including the hashtag and report both length counts.
