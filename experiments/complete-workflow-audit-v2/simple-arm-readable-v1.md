# Simple arm readable causes — v2

These readable descriptions are the semantic source of truth for candidate render 1. Compression may shorten constants and syntax, but the named relationships below must remain causal for Grade 1.

| Brief | Defining cause / relationships |
| --- | --- |
| F01 | Open axial sampling `u∈[-1,1]`; off-center rational envelope centered near `u=.2`; a traveling sine phase multiplied by that envelope. |
| F02 | Open axial sampling; cancellation node `v(t)` migrates; amplitude is proportional to distance `|u-v|`; a traveling carrier wave remains on both sides. |
| F03 | Open axial sampling; low-frequency traveling carrier plus higher-frequency counter-moving corrugation; fine-wave amplitude varies with axial position. |
| F04 | Open axial sampling; persistent low-frequency S-like backbone independent of time; higher-frequency ripple travels and is strongest away from the endpoints. |
| F05 | Open axial sampling; traveling wave plus continuous bounded sign-change term `v/(ε+|v|)`; the sign-change center migrates with time. |
| F06 | Open axial sampling; primary traveling wave has amplitude increasing toward the ends; a smaller higher-frequency counter-moving wave remains global. |
| F07 | Open axial sampling; quadratic envelope produces a low-amplitude center and active ends; traveling carrier phase moves through the whole line. |
| F08 | Open axial sampling; amplitude envelope has a time-varying linear tilt in `u`; the carrier wave travels while energy shifts between sides. |
| F09 | Open axial sampling; persistent broad backbone plus a localized rational packet; packet center moves while a high-frequency phase travels inside it. |
| F10 | Open axial sampling; two nearby spatial frequencies with different temporal rates interfere to produce a changing beat envelope. |
| F11 | Open axial sampling; phase contains both linear and quadratic terms in `u`, giving a spatial chirp; a time offset advances the phase globally. |
| F12 | Open axial sampling; low-frequency static backbone and much higher-frequency traveling corrugation are summed, preserving two structural scales. |

All candidates intentionally use a single point-sampled spine and therefore cannot satisfy the brief by becoming a closed loop, radial flower, sheet, or branched morphology.
