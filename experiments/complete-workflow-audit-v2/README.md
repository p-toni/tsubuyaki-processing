# Complete workflow audit v2 — filament-aligned

## Why v2 exists

v1 stopped before the research arm because its `recurrence` briefs required a stateful iterated-map cause while the autonomous prototype route named `recurrence` implements a different non-stateful axial/spine grammar. Relaxing that contract after seeing the S arm would have biased R.

v2 removes that confound rather than patching it post hoc. All 12 fresh briefs use **intentional filament**: an open one-dimensional axial topology whose semantics and hard-validity contract align between the simplified skill and the supported autonomous prototype.

Frozen base: `b8cb5d26853f380f70746bdb9613512d47d55056`.

## Decision this experiment can change

Does the strongest currently supported research workflow improve **finished, verified #つぶやきProcessing filament posts** enough to justify its added machinery over the simplified ordinary skill workflow?

The primary outcome is the final animation a blinded human reviewer would choose to keep. Candidate count, runtime, archive coverage and mechanical validity are explanatory only.

## Arms

### S — simplified ordinary workflow

Follow frozen `SKILL.md` literally:

```text
choose the smallest suitable filament representation
→ build one viable readable sketch
→ inspect frozen temporal horizon
→ explore only for a concrete shortcoming or worthwhile alternative
→ select visually
→ compress preserving named cause + motion
→ verify exact post
```

No autonomous archive, mixed-material search, structural target, max-dispersion reducer or research selector may influence S.

All 12 S final posts and their stop/revise ledger must be committed before R generation exists.

### R — strongest supported filament research workflow

For the same brief:

```text
one hard-valid filament start
→ exactly 20 generated attempts
   10 native + 10 generic spectral
→ complete hard-valid archive
→ target-blind raw-pixel max-dispersion three-item shortlist
→ visual/temporal selection among those three against the brief
→ readable cause record for the selected expanded candidate
→ compression-survival promotion/fallback
→ exact final verification
```

Only the 1-D mixed material control and max-dispersion delivery surfaces with prospective artistic support are used. Restart sidecars, learned judges, late-refinement policies, family projected-spectral and recurrent learned operators are excluded.

## Comparable ceilings

Per brief and arm:

- at most 20 pre-compression candidate renders;
- at most 2 deployment compression attempts;
- same frozen temporal horizon for selection and final verification.

S may stop early; unused candidate budget is recorded. R uses the frozen 20-attempt allocation.

## Final artifact contract

Every reviewable final post must:

1. have a readable source/cause record;
2. be exact executable p5.js global mode ending `//#つぶやきProcessing`;
3. fit both <=280 raw code points and <=280 X-weighted characters;
4. execute over the full frozen temporal horizon;
5. retain open axial filament identity (Grade 0);
6. preserve the selected candidate's named defining relationships (Grade 1).

If the artistic winner cannot survive compression, the arm may use the next already-generated visually worthwhile candidate, within the two-attempt compression ceiling. The expanded artistic winner remains recorded separately.

## Primary advancement gate

`RESEARCH_WORKFLOW_FINISHED_ARTIFACT_SUPPORT_FILAMENT` requires all:

1. >=10/12 reviewable pairs;
2. >=8 decisive pairs;
3. R decisive win rate >0.65;
4. one-sided exact binomial p<=0.10 against p=0.5;
5. R verified-deployment completion rate >= S.

Failure leaves the research runtime optional for ordinary filament work.

## Bottleneck audit

After final ratings are fixed, each brief may be classified:

- `GENERATION_REPRESENTATION`: neither workflow exposes a compelling candidate, or good filament structure stalls under available transitions;
- `SELECTION_DELIVERY`: compelling generated R candidate exists but delivery/selection misses it;
- `COMPRESSION_REPRESENTATION`: selected expanded candidate is compelling but its defining identity cannot survive <=280;
- `WORKFLOW_STOPPING`: final quality is comparable while R consumes materially more candidate/compute effort;
- `NO_SINGLE_BOTTLENECK`.

Uninspected archive members cannot receive artistic labels by proxy. Full-archive artistic inspection happens only after primary final-artifact ratings, and only when needed to diagnose a missed opportunity.

The parked recurrent learned-operator branch may reopen only if this audit specifically supports `GENERATION_REPRESENTATION` and indicates that existing transitions—not selection or compression—are the limiting factor.
