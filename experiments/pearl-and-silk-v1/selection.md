## Selected sketch

**V2, provisional B.** At frame 45 the pearl sits in a small pocket to the right. By frame 90 the pocket has widened and deepened, and the two sides carry visibly different slopes. As the pearl comes free on the left, both ends hold and the silk hangs in a shallow bow. Wider spacing lets the eye follow individual threads as they bunch under the pearl. The material remains legible in the quiet half of the cycle. The silk settles immediately on release and the second orbit repeats the first. That limits the sense of weight after contact.
