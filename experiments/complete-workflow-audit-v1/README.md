# Complete workflow audit v1

## Decision this experiment can change

Does the strongest currently supported research workflow improve **finished, verified #つぶやきProcessing posts** enough to justify its added machinery over the simplified ordinary skill workflow?

This is the first-priority study from `references/research-roadmap.md` at base commit `b8cb5d26853f380f70746bdb9613512d47d55056`.

The primary outcome is the final animation a blinded human reviewer would choose to keep. Mechanical validity, archive coverage, candidate count, elapsed effort and compression fallback are explanatory measurements only.

## Frozen scope

The study uses 12 fresh briefs, six recurrence and six intentional-filament briefs. These routes are chosen because the intrinsic-1D native/spectral mixed search and target-blind three-item max-dispersion delivery are the strongest research surfaces with prospective artistic support.

The study intentionally excludes:

- restart sidecar promotion or substitution;
- family projected-spectral as a default artistic intervention;
- semantic/model/morphology artistic judges;
- late-refinement heuristics;
- learned world-model/navigation machinery;
- the parked recurrent learned-operator branch.

No closed population is reused as optimizer input.

## The two workflows

### S — simplified ordinary skill

Follow `SKILL.md` from the frozen base literally:

```text
choose the smallest suitable representation
→ build one viable readable sketch
→ inspect representative times
→ explore only if a specific shortcoming or worthwhile alternative is visible
→ select visually
→ compress while preserving named cause + motion
→ verify the exact post
```

No autonomous-prototype archive, structural recovery target, max-dispersion reducer or research selector may influence this arm.

All 12 S artifacts are frozen **before** any R-arm candidate package is generated or inspected.

### R — strongest supported research workflow

For the same brief and route:

```text
one hard-valid start
→ 20 generated attempts
   10 native + 10 generic spectral
→ complete hard-valid archive
→ target-blind raw-pixel max-dispersion three-item shortlist
→ visual/temporal selection among those three
→ compression-survival promotion/fallback
→ exact final verification
```

The research machinery may propose alternatives but does not receive automatic artistic authority. Final selection from the three-item delivery set is visual/temporal.

## Comparable total budget

Each arm receives the same hard ceilings per brief:

- **20 pre-compression candidate renders**;
- **2 full compression attempts**;
- one frozen temporal observation contract for selection, preflight and final verification.

S may stop before 20 if the incumbent is already strong; unused budget is recorded rather than spent artificially. R uses its frozen 20-attempt research allocation. Candidate renders, compression attempts and elapsed execution effort are reported as secondary efficiency outcomes.

No arm may exceed the ceiling to rescue a weak result.

## Final artifact contract

Every reviewable arm must produce:

1. readable source identifying the mathematical cause;
2. exact executable p5.js global-mode post ending in `//#つぶやきProcessing`;
3. weighted X length <=280 and raw Unicode code points <=280;
4. exact-code runtime pass;
5. frozen meaningful temporal horizon with matched review frames;
6. Grade 0 observable survival across that horizon;
7. Grade 1 preservation of the named defining relationships.

A length pass alone is insufficient.

If the visual winner cannot survive compression, the workflow may use its next already-generated visually worthwhile candidate, within the two-compression-attempt ceiling. The artistic winner remains recorded separately from the deployment finalist.

## Fresh briefs

The exact 12 briefs are frozen in `briefs.json`. They are intentionally phrased as desired animated behavior + topology rather than target images or descriptor vectors.

Routes:

```text
B01–B06 recurrence
B07–B12 filament
```

No brief is derived from a consumed human-review block.

## Arm ordering / contamination control

1. preregister protocol and briefs;
2. complete and commit all S-arm final artifacts and ledgers;
3. source-lock S-arm files;
4. only then generate/inspect R-arm archives;
5. complete and commit all R-arm final artifacts and ledgers;
6. build blinded A/B reviewer package with a sealed random identity key;
7. commit all human ratings before opening the key;
8. score the frozen gate.

This ordering prevents the research archive from suggesting forms to the ordinary arm.

## Human question

For each brief:

> Which final verified post is the stronger mathematical animation worth keeping?

Allowed judgments:

```text
A>B
B>A
equivalent
unreviewable
```

The reviewer sees matched temporal frames from the exact final post plus the brief. Workflow identity, effort and lineage are hidden.

## Primary advancement gate

`RESEARCH_WORKFLOW_FINISHED_ARTIFACT_SUPPORT` requires all:

1. >=10/12 reviewable pairs;
2. >=8 decisive pairs;
3. R decisive win rate >0.65;
4. one-sided exact binomial p<=0.10 against p=0.5;
5. R does not have a lower verified-deployment completion rate than S.

If the artistic gate fails, the research runtime remains optional even if it improves mechanical or archive measures.

## Bottleneck audit

Independently of the primary gate, every brief is classified after final artifacts exist:

| Observation | Classification |
| --- | --- |
| neither workflow exposes a compelling expanded candidate | `GENERATION_REPRESENTATION` |
| a compelling generated candidate exists but is missed by that workflow's delivery/selection path | `SELECTION_DELIVERY` |
| selected expanded candidate is compelling but cannot preserve identity under the final-post boundary | `COMPRESSION_REPRESENTATION` |
| both reach comparable final quality but R consumes materially more work/compute | `WORKFLOW_STOPPING` |
| no clear failure pattern | `NO_SINGLE_BOTTLENECK` |

An archive audit can identify a missed opportunity only when a candidate was actually inspected after the primary human ratings are fixed; uninspected archive members are never labeled artistically poor by proxy.

## Decision consequences

### Positive

If the primary gate passes, identify which stage produced the gain before proposing a broader production change. Do not generalize automatically across 2-D or morphology routes.

### Negative

If the primary gate fails, keep the research runtime experimental/optional and prioritize improving the simplified workflow at the diagnosed bottleneck.

### Recurrent branch

The recurrent learned-operator branch may reopen only if this audit specifically indicates `GENERATION_REPRESENTATION` and the evidence suggests good representations stall because available transitions cannot make useful changes. It does not reopen merely because R loses or costs more.
