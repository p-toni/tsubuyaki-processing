# multiplex-capacity-v1

Status: **preregistered, not executed**.

This experiment is the gate between the Yuruyurau grammar audit and any proposal for a sixth representation.

## Claim under test

A neutral **index-multiplexed latent geometry** primitive provides structural search capacity that is not efficiently reachable by the current five routes under equal budget.

The intervention is mechanistic. It must not port any source formula, reproduce a reference image, or encode named creature semantics.

## Experimental representation

The candidate grammar must contain these independently ablatable mechanisms:

- `slow`: a low-frequency coordinate from one dense scalar sample index;
- `fast`: one or more high-frequency coordinates from the same index;
- `branch`: a small categorical residue coordinate derived from that index;
- `latent`: a nonlinear radius/energy variable built from slow + fast coordinates;
- `reuse`: the latent variable affects at least two distinct downstream roles among phase, amplitude, deformation, and motion;
- `cross`: multiplicative coupling between slow, fast, latent, and time terms;
- `singular`: optional bounded reciprocal response with an explicit denominator floor.

The genome must expose mechanisms as parameters rather than hard-code one target family.

## Required ablations

Evaluate the full grammar against four ablations, all with equal genome dimensionality where practical and equal candidate-evaluation budget:

1. **no-branch** — categorical residue coordinate removed;
2. **regular-grid** — replace single-index fast/slow multiplexing with an explicit smooth two-dimensional lattice;
3. **no-reuse** — latent radius may place points but cannot also drive phase/deformation/motion;
4. **no-singular** — bounded reciprocal mechanism removed.

The current five routes are a fifth comparator group and remain unchanged.

## Challenge set

Use neutral, synthetic mechanism challenges rather than copies of public artworks.

Freeze 12 challenge generators before opening results:

- 3 require multiple phase-linked submanifolds without explicit organ anchors;
- 3 require woven/pseudo-2D structure from one dense index;
- 3 require latent-radius coupling between shape and motion;
- 3 require combinations of the above with controlled localized high-curvature detail.

At least four challenges must be deliberately designed so a smooth sheet or explicit family hierarchy is a plausible solution. This prevents an unfair candidate-only target set.

Challenge generators must be structurally distinct from the candidate representation implementation: they may share mechanism labels but not the candidate's coordinate equations.

## Population

First pass uses already-consumed master seeds only. No fresh search evidence is spent in v1.

Use 20 consumed master seeds, all five fixed current-route strata, candidate multiplex, and all four ablations.

No early stopping, seed replacement, or post-hoc target substitution.

## Search budget

Equal candidate-evaluation budget per representation × seed × challenge.

Do not use representation-specific mutation counts, privileged starts, or route-specific target knowledge.

The existing confirmed repertoire-preserving allocator may be used only if every comparator receives the same allocator contract. Otherwise use the simpler common search topology.

## Measurement

Primary: normalized sparse-geometry-v1 target recovery on complete master seeds, averaging challenge effects before seed inference.

Secondary:

- `structural-v1` niche coverage across hard-valid candidates;
- unique rendered phenotype rate;
- hard-valid yield;
- per-challenge and per-representation means as diagnostics;
- leave-one-challenge-family-out and leave-one-seed-out means;
- largest representation × challenge × seed cell.

Inference is over complete master seeds, not representation × seed pseudo-replicates.

## Pilot advancement gate

`MULTIPLEX_CAPACITY_PROMISING` iff all hard invariants pass and all of the following hold:

1. full multiplex mean recovery advantage over **best current-route result per seed/challenge** is > 0;
2. every leave-one-challenge-family-out mean of that advantage is > 0;
3. full multiplex mean recovery advantage over each ablation is > 0;
4. full multiplex produces at least 1.25× the distinct `structural-v1` niches of its strongest ablation after normalizing by hard-valid count;
5. no single challenge family contributes more than 60% of the aggregate positive advantage.

This is a consumed-seed pilot only. Passing it does not admit a new route.

## If promising

1. freeze the exact multiplex genome, mutator, checker, search topology, targets, and reducer;
2. power-plan a fresh fixed-sample confirmation;
3. run fresh confirmation exactly once;
4. only after mechanical confirmation, run a blind artistic portfolio comparison against the five-route repertoire;
5. representation admission still requires evidence that the new route adds a niche not already preserved by current routes, and does not weaken outer route authority.

## If not promising

Do not retune the same mechanism family against these 20 consumed seeds. Classify which assumption failed and either close the line or define a materially new v2 hypothesis with a different consumed holdout.

## Relationship to the current research program

This experiment runs in parallel with the independent artistic replication of the confirmed repertoire allocator. It does not modify #79, reinterpret its blind evidence, or change the confirmed allocator.
