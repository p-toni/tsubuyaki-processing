The protocol and all 24 batch sources were committed before study renders.

## Initial shared representations, before observation

- F01: two folded surfaces share a separation variable and change their material
  with it. Risk: overlap may read as two objects crossing rather than one splitting.
- F02: crossing ribbons carry depth coloring and a common traveling warp. Risk:
  shading alone may not make alternating over/under relationships legible.
- F03: a localized wave packet moves back and forth across an anchored sheet.
  Risk: the carrier's motion may obscure the reflection of its envelope.
- F04: the two bodies and bridge use the same delayed phase. Risk: continuous
  oscillation may hide the passage and reception of a distinct influence.
- F05: a ruled sheet twists increasingly away from its top edge. Risk: transparent
  points may show overlap without readable front/back occlusion.
- F06: lanes bend around a central clearance and recover downstream. Risk:
  equally spaced points may make the flow look static despite coherent paths.

All planned batch alternatives are recorded in `batch-commitment.json`. No
rendered observation has yet informed them. Only the adaptive arm can revise.
