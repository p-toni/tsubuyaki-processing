# Discovery decisions

Protocol and briefs were frozen before creating candidates. Each version below is
preserved. Decisions are authored by the same model that constructs the work.

## Initial representations — before rendering

- B01/v1: choose a sampled ring surface because two coordinates can expose a
  persistent cavity and translucent overlapping material. Couple a breathing tube
  radius with a traveling fold. Risk: it may read as a decorative torus.
- B02/v1: choose seven related axial fronds. Shared root, bend amplitude growing
  toward the tip, traveling phase, and a narrow deterministic width field should
  preserve attachment while allowing differentiated motion. Risk: thin ribbons
  may overlap or read as simple lines.
- B03/v1: choose indexed particles with radial phase delay and differential
  angular motion. This should make gathering coherent without rigid rotation.
  Risk: a uniform pulsating disk may have too little internal identity.

## First visual review — sixteen frames each, plus full-size B01/B03 stills

- B01/v1: the cavity persists and the double surface forms distinct silky folds.
  The changing rim and tube breathing are visible across the horizon. Retain this
  as a provisional B incumbent: a coherent study, although its toroidal symmetry
  limits originality. No revision is justified merely to spend the allowance.
- B02/v1: the common root and traveling bends are clear, but outer tips approach
  or cross the horizontal edges. The shape is too stretched and thin. Propose v2:
  shorten the shared length from 250 to 210, move the root from y=350 to 320 to
  recenter the body, and widen the narrow material band from 8 to 12. Preserve the
  bend law and all seven family identities. This is a composition/material repair.
- B03/v1: gathering and release work, and differential turning produces visible
  spiral bands. However, the silhouette remains a generic disk/ring. Provisional
  C. Propose v2: add three angular phase cohorts to the gathering law, so different
  sectors gather at different times. Preserve particle identities and radial delay.
  This tests structural organization rather than adding unrelated decoration.

## Second visual review and selection — before compression

- B02/v2: the fronds have clear margins and the widened bands read as flexible
  material. The root remains fixed while different bends travel upward. Promote
  v2 over v1; provisional B. Limit: the form is still very regular and shares a
  repeated gesture across its members. No third candidate is justified now.
- B03/v2: three lobes emerge during contraction and dissolve into a circulating
  point field. This gives the gathering a visible organization absent in v1.
  Promote v2; provisional B with lower confidence. Limit: the fine points are faint
  at thumbnail scale, and radial ornament remains a strong part of its identity.
- Select B01/v1, B02/v2, and B03/v2. These are studies worth keeping, not A-grade
  finished works. Grade and selection precede compact character counting.

## Relationships to preserve

- B01: two-coordinate sampled ring surface, persistent central cavity, sinusoidal
  tube breathing, and a fold traveling through the shared surface coordinates.
- B02: seven axial families with a common root, bend amplitude increasing toward
  the tip, member phase differences, and width vanishing at both ends.
- B03: stable particle identities, radial phase delay, differential angular motion,
  and three angular gathering cohorts. No random reseeding between frames.

## Delivery review and infrastructure repairs

- All first compact attempts pass both character counts. Full-horizon geometry
  is identical to each selected readable source; all 48 sampled PNG pairs match.
  Final contact-sheet review retains provisional B for all three, with lower
  confidence for Gather. No artwork source changed after selection/compression.
- Every archived browser render uses pinned full p5.js. An unused lightweight
  runtime copy was removed from the delivery package. The render wrapper copies
  a supplied text post to a JavaScript file so browser MIME handling is correct.
- The first gallery control check exposed one extra scheduled draw after Pause.
  Guarding the gallery wrapper while paused fixed it. A second check passed
  actual complete playback, pause, restart, and replay for all three sketches.
  This changed presentation controls, not the exact posts or discovery results.
- Final renderer review found that sparse sampling skipped intermediate calls.
  This was safe for the stateless readable previews used here, but would advance
  a compact post's counter incorrectly. Sparse capture now executes all frames
  and only saves the selected ones. A compact Gather rerender is compared against
  the already completed sequential clip frames; historical render logs remain
  unchanged. This repair neither introduces a new candidate nor changes selection.
