# Compact representation v1 — prospective protocol

## Decision and scope

Can a compact representation retain both a difficult visual behavior and the
artistic quality of the best readable candidate within a small fixed budget?

This is one fresh engineering case, not a timing comparison, statistical test,
independent artistic validation, or general reliability estimate. The same agent
authors and reviews both representations in shared context. It tests whether a
useful complete construction exists on this brief; it cannot establish a
generally superior representation or change the production default.

## Fresh brief: Pulse vessel

A single opaque soft vessel has two rounded chambers joined by a visibly
continuous narrow waist. A localized squeeze travels from one chamber, through
the waist, into the other, then returns. Material behind the squeeze relaxes.
The two chambers remain part of one body throughout. The body deforms in place:
global scaling, rigid motion, unrelated flashing, a decorative traveling stripe,
disconnected blobs, or an apparent break at the waist do not satisfy the brief.

Require readable material volume and coherent composition in addition to those
causal relationships. No exact biological anatomy or physical simulation is
claimed. Do not reuse candidates from any closed study.

## Representations and budget

- **R1, swept surface:** directly define the radius of a continuous axial body
  and deform that radius with a traveling local contraction. Sample its visible
  surface and shade its volume.
- **R2, implicit field:** define the body through a joint distance field of two
  stationary centers; apply a localized change to that shared field and derive
  visible material from it. It must remain connected. Replacing this field with
  R1's radius envelope would abandon the representation and is outside budget.

At most **two readable candidates and two compact attempts per representation**:
four readable programs and four attempted posts in total. A behavioral or style
change consumes a readable candidate. A complete compact program written for
testing consumes an attempt, even if it fails syntax or length. No hidden drafts,
parameter sweeps, uncharged golf attempts, or new candidates after selection.

Publish this protocol before candidate construction, and publish both initial
sources before rendering. Render and inspect both initial candidates, then use
at most one observation-led revision per representation if useful. Stop sooner
when no concrete worthwhile improvement remains.

Freeze all readable grades, requirements, the selected candidate per
representation, and the preferred readable incumbent before any compact source
or character count. An explicit equivalent or uncertain preference is allowed.
Only an A/B candidate with all brief requirements met is eligible for compression.
Retain failed candidates and the stronger readable incumbent even if it cannot
be compacted. Compact work may alter syntax/algebra but cannot change the chosen
readable behavior, material, sampling, or styling. No fallback beyond the frozen
eligible selection.

## Review and technical contract

Use full p5.js 1.11.3 at 400×400, frames 1–720 with a 60 fps frame clock
(twelve seconds). The squeeze must visit both chambers and pass through the
waist during this interval; the interval cannot change to rescue a failure.
Review sixteen evenly spaced stills and full-size frames where relationships
are unclear. Separate visual judgments from numerical and playback checks.

A = distinctive finished work; B = coherent interesting study worth keeping;
C = functioning demonstration; D = broken or incoherent. For each candidate,
record requirements as met, unmet, or uncertain. Uncertainty does not count as
adherence. A length pass cannot upgrade a grade. Preserve any clear differences
in volume, material, composition, and expressive motion in the pair judgment.

Require finite coordinates/styles, a stable point count and indexed identity,
at least 95% of point centers in frame, and maximum point motion of 10 pixels
per frame. There is no recycling exception. These are technical constraints,
not artistic ranking criteria.

Every delivered complete post, including its hashtag, must fit both 280 raw
Unicode code points and 280 X-weighted characters. Verify its exact source in
the browser with no errors over all 720 frames. Require readable/compact
geometry and style agreement within 1e-7 every frame and byte-identical PNGs at
the sixteen sampled times in the same raster environment. Manually verify the
named causes survive. A mathematical match or stable playback does not establish
artistic quality or guarantee native 60 fps performance.

## Success, effort, and closure

The case succeeds only if at least one verified, brief-adherent A/B delivery has
**no clear artistic loss against the frozen best readable incumbent**. If that
comparison is uncertain, record the case as inconclusive rather than promoting
it. A technically successful but artistically weaker delivery does not pass.

If the case passes, the next decision is whether to replicate on another fresh
brief; no universal policy follows. If it fails, classify the observed obstacle
and stop this population. Do not tune its candidates or grades afterward.

Record every candidate, attempt, failure, render, source hash, and known execution
cost. Distinguish physical from logical work if evidence is reused. Report human
creative interventions and unmeasured reasoning/review effort. Equal caps do not
establish equal cost. Infrastructure repairs are allowed, must be recorded, and
do not extend creative budgets. Freeze results before updating documentation.
