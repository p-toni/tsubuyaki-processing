# Route-conditional policy v1 — fresh-seed holdout

## Question

Can the universal staged adaptive topology be replaced by a **simpler route-only policy choice** without knowing whether the hidden target is local or global?

This follows `search-leverage-v1`, which found that the current staged topology is not generally supported as a representation-agnostic default.

The experiment is objective search-mechanics evidence only. It does not evaluate artistic quality.

## Train / holdout separation

Training evidence is frozen from `search-leverage-v1` workflow run `33210657342` on seeds:

```text
101, 103, 107
```

Holdout seeds are fresh and were not used to choose any policy:

```text
109, 113, 127
```

The complete training derivation is frozen in `training-mapping.json` before holdout execution.

## No target-regime oracle

The policy chooser receives **representation identity only**.

It does not receive:

- the hidden rendered target;
- target distance;
- whether the target was generated by the local or global regime;
- holdout performance.

One policy is frozen per representation and used unchanged for both target regimes and all holdout seeds.

Internal candidate selection within a benchmark policy still uses the same target-distance objective as `search-leverage-v1`; the anti-oracle constraint applies to choosing the **search topology**, not to the objective being optimized by that topology.

## Training rule

Only the two simple policies are eligible for route selection:

```text
independent-breadth
fixed-parent-local
```

For each training route × seed:

1. take each simple policy's normalized improvement on the local target;
2. take its normalized improvement on the global target;
3. average the two values;
4. the strict higher combined score wins that seed.

The route selects the simple policy winning at least `2/3` training seeds. A tie or lack of majority would leave the route unresolved rather than inventing a choice.

Frozen mapping:

```text
recurrence → fixed-parent-local
orbit      → fixed-parent-local
family     → independent-breadth
sheet      → fixed-parent-local
filament   → fixed-parent-local
```

Across all 15 training route × seed blocks, fixed-parent won the same simple-policy comparison `9/15`, versus breadth `6/15`, so the frozen **universal simple control** is:

```text
fixed-parent-local
```

This makes the route-conditioning claim narrow and falsifiable: the mapping differs from the universal simple control only on `family`.

## Holdout workload

The holdout reuses the exact objective and generation mechanics from `search-leverage-v1` with new seeds:

```text
routes                     recurrence, orbit, family, sheet, filament
holdout seeds              109, 113, 127
common starts              3
matched frames             30, 90, 150
local target steps         6 accepted mutations
local target scale         0.65
explore_per_basin          4
roundA_per_survivor        3
total_extra_budget         8
```

For every route × seed × target regime, all three policies are rerun under equal incremental candidate-evaluation budgets:

```text
adaptive
independent-breadth
fixed-parent-local
```

Invalid candidates consume budget. The route-selected result is then read from the already-frozen mapping; no holdout result can change the selected policy.

## Primary score

For a policy in one route × seed block:

```text
combinedScore = mean(
  local normalized improvement,
  global normalized improvement
)
```

Averaging both regimes forces one route policy to handle both kinds of hidden target. The target regime cannot choose the topology.

### Simplification gate

For each holdout seed:

```text
route-selected simple combinedScore >= adaptive combinedScore
```

Exact equality counts as non-worse because the selected policy is structurally simpler; any objective loss larger than numerical epsilon fails that seed.

A route supports replacement when the selected simple policy is non-worse on at least `2/3` fresh seeds.

Across representations:

```text
4–5 supporting routes → general route-only simplification supported
3 supporting routes   → mixed / representation-dependent simplification
0–2 supporting routes → general simplification not supported
```

Strict wins (`selected > adaptive`) are recorded separately. They are stronger evidence but are not required for a complexity-reducing replacement.

### Route-conditioning gate

Because the trained route map differs from universal fixed-parent only on `family`, route-conditioning itself has one clean holdout test:

```text
family breadth combinedScore > family fixed-parent combinedScore
```

The `family→breadth` exception generalizes only if breadth wins strictly on at least `2/3` fresh family seeds.

This gate cannot be rescued by results on the four routes where the policies are identical.

## Secondary evidence

Record, but do not let override the primary rules:

- strict selected-simple wins over adaptive;
- universal fixed-parent vs adaptive;
- local/global regime-specific means;
- valid yield;
- exact rendered-phenotype uniqueness;
- adaptive lineage depth;
- oracle best-of-three gap, as a diagnostic ceiling only.

## Predeclared decision

If route-only simplification passes on at least `4/5` routes, the next implementation experiment may simplify the search topology behind an opt-in research flag.

If simplification passes but the `family→breadth` exception fails, prefer the universal simple fixed-parent hypothesis over route conditioning.

If simplification fails, retain current adaptive search as the research baseline and investigate a different simple policy family rather than adding more stages to the existing topology.

No result in this experiment authorizes artistic promotion, hard representation pruning, or a production `SKILL.md` change.
