# Blind Evaluation Scorecard

Do not reveal A/B/C condition labels during evaluation.

For each finalist, record the dimensions independently. Prefer pairwise choices for aesthetics; use the ordinal fields mainly for analysis and disagreement inspection.

## 1. Brief adherence

`0–4`

- 0 — misses the requested topology/intent
- 1 — weak resemblance; major constraint violated
- 2 — partially satisfies brief
- 3 — clearly satisfies brief
- 4 — unusually strong realization of the brief

## 2. Aesthetic preference

Pairwise question:

> If only one candidate could be kept for another hour of exploration, which one would you keep?

Optional `0–4` confidence only; do not interpret it as an absolute beauty score.

## 3. Distinctiveness

`0–4`

Does it occupy a meaningfully different visual/mathematical niche from the nearby archive rather than being a parameter-neighbor?

## 4. Mathematical leverage

`0–4`

How disproportionate is the phenotype to the mathematical cause?

High leverage means a small coherent rule set generates structure that would have been expensive to specify directly.

## 5. Temporal quality

`0–4`

- internal transformation rather than rigid movement;
- coherent identity over time;
- useful rhythm / phase relationships;
- no frame-specific lucky accident.

## 6. Tweet viability

`0–4`

How likely is the defining generator to survive a <=280 weighted-character compilation?

Judge representation leverage, not current expanded-source length.

## 7. Keep / reject reason

One sentence identifying the strongest causal reason for keeping or rejecting the candidate.

# Pairwise protocol

For each brief:

1. select one finalist from each condition without revealing labels;
2. randomize the three ids;
3. compare all three pairs on **aesthetic preference**;
4. separately score adherence, distinctiveness, leverage, temporal quality, tweet viability;
5. reveal conditions only after judgments are frozen.

Do not resolve disagreement by averaging everything into one score. Inspect which dimension caused the disagreement.

# Anti-Goodhart rules

- descriptor novelty is not aesthetic quality;
- occupancy is not adherence for filament/multi-instance modes;
- a structurally novel candidate that violates the brief does not count as a C win;
- a candidate that looks good at one frame but collapses temporally does not count as a strong finalist;
- validators are falsification tools, not beauty functions.
