# Epistemic Assumptions Under Test

This file exists to prevent the experiment from quietly turning current beliefs into permanent architecture.

## Goal

Reliably discover **original, aesthetically compelling mathematical systems whose visual/temporal complexity is disproportionately larger than their description**, and that can plausibly compile into #つぶやきProcessing.

Controllability is conditional. It is valuable when requested, but it is not synonymous with artistic quality.

## Evidence we trust reasonably well

From published examples and our v0.2–v0.6 tests:

- few latent variables can control many visible effects;
- modulo/parity can behave like a family/developmental variable;
- nonlinear deformation + projection creates much of the apparent complexity;
- recurrence can be used as latent material instead of literal attractor coordinates;
- math-first and morphology-first are both legitimate representations;
- explicit raster validators are useful for falsification but do not create aesthetic quality;
- 280 weighted characters constrains representation, but is not currently the main discovery bottleneck.

## Major unknowns

### U1 — representation vs search

How much quality variance is explained by choosing the right representation versus exploring many candidates inside a fixed representation?

This is the primary purpose of the A/B/C experiment.

### U2 — parameter vs structural exploration

Are good regions mostly reachable through numeric tuning, or are they separated by discrete changes in kernel/deformation/projection/family structure?

### U3 — selection function

Published examples are **selected winners**. We do not observe the discarded equations, sweeps, accidents, or curation that preceded them.

Do not infer an artist's generation process from a final one-liner without evidence.

### U4 — temporal quality

Static-frame visual quality may be a weak proxy for animated identity, coherent transformation, rhythm and surprise.

### U5 — emergence vs editability

Explicit morphology may improve local editing while reducing representational leverage. The likely optimum may be an indirect mathematical core plus sparse explicit constraints.

## Priors to avoid hard-coding

- "better" does not mean "more controllable";
- "more diverse" does not mean "better";
- "higher occupancy" does not mean "better" across all modes;
- morphology is not the universal form of complexity;
- image-space semantics are not always the best source of truth;
- published examples do not reveal search effort;
- one successful cold test is not strong evidence of general improvement;
- there may be many qualitatively different high-value niches rather than one optimum.

## Falsification outcomes

The experiment should be able to convince us of any of these:

1. **A ≈ B ≈ C** — stop building search infrastructure; improve representation/model reasoning.
2. **B ≈ C > A** — parameter exploration is the high-leverage intervention.
3. **C > B > A** — structural divergent search is justified.
4. **winner depends strongly on brief** — invest in route-specific search rather than one universal loop.
5. **novel C outputs lose on adherence/quality** — our structural grammar is too unconstrained or descriptors reward task drift.

The purpose is to reduce uncertainty, not to prove structural search is correct.
